01

Draw a card.
Take a moment.

Drag the deck down or tap Draw a Card, then tap the card to turn it over. Each card pairs an affirmation with a color theme. Take a moment to read it, save it for later, or simply get on with your day.

02

Look back,
in color.

If you like, record how you feel after drawing a card. Your entries build a seven-color energy ring and a calendar you can look back through at your own pace. Here, “energy” means your own sense of how you feel, not a health measurement. Your draw history and preferences stay on your device.

03

Keep a little
color nearby.

Keep your latest card or energy ring in view with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. You can also set an optional reminder in Hue. Reminders are scheduled on your device; iOS manages widget refresh timing.

04

Quiet by design

Private by default

No account is required, and your draw history and preferences stay on your device. Hue has no third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Optional feedback is covered separately in our Privacy Policy.

Four languages

Enjoy Hue in English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese. All four languages are available in the free app.
