App launch · iPhone

A daily pause,
guided by color.

Hue turns drawing a card into a brief, tactile ritual for noticing the present moment—without an account, a subscription, or cross-app tracking.

  • 88 color-inspired cards
  • 4 launch languages
  • Home & Lock Screen widgets
  • One-time Hue Pro purchase

01

A physical-deck-inspired ritual

Drag the deck down or tap the button to draw. A card arrives with a color theme and an affirmation; swipe it to reveal the reverse. The interaction is intentionally brief and can end there—there is no required account or forced check-in.

02

Color becomes a private history

After a draw, people can record how the moment feels. Hue turns those entries into a seven-color energy ring and a calendar for gentle reflection. Draw history, saved cards, reminders, and preferences remain on the device.

03

A pause beyond the app

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets keep the latest card or energy ring close by. Optional reminders are scheduled locally, and iOS controls when each widget refreshes.

04

Quiet by design

Private by default

No account is required. Personal reflection data stays on the device. Hue has no third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Four languages at launch

English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese are supported throughout the interface and all 88 cards.

No subscription

Hue Pro is a one-time purchase for the current core benefits. Optional tips do not unlock features.
