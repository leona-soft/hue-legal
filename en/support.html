Hue Support

For help, feedback, or a privacy request, email leona.soft@icloud.com. Include your app version, iOS version, and a short description, but do not send passwords, payment information, or sensitive health information.

Feedback is not sending

In-app feedback uses Apple CloudKit. Confirm that you are signed in to iCloud and have a network connection, then try again. You can always use the email address above instead.

Restore Hue Pro

Open Settings, select Hue Pro, then choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the lifetime purchase. Hue Pro is a one-time, non-renewing purchase.

Local data

Draws, likes, bookmarks, energy records, reminders, and settings are stored locally. Removing the app may remove this data. Hue does not currently provide account sync or cloud backup for draw history.

Notifications and widgets

Check notification permission in iOS Settings if reminders do not appear. If a widget looks stale, open Hue once and wait for the system to refresh it; iOS controls the exact refresh time.

Privacy and deletion

Read the Privacy Policy. To request early deletion of submitted feedback, email us with the optional email address or approximate submission time used for the original feedback.
