Hue Privacy Policy

Effective: August 12, 2026

1. Local data

Draw history, likes, bookmarks, energy records, reminders, and app settings are stored locally using Core Data or UserDefaults. Limited widget state is shared between Hue and its widget through Hue’s App Group. Draw data is not uploaded to a personal iCloud database.

2. In-app feedback

Only when you submit feedback, Hue sends the feedback text, optional email address, app version and build, iOS version, device model, and locale to the public CloudKit database in iCloud.com.leonasoft.hue. CloudKit attaches an account identifier scoped to that container.

Production permissions allow signed-in iCloud users to create feedback records only; app clients cannot read, change, or delete feedback.

In-app feedback may be submitted only by users who are 14 or older. Users under 14 must not submit information through in-app feedback and should ask a parent or guardian to email leona.soft@icloud.com. The age confirmation is checked locally before submission; Hue does not store or upload age.

3. Purpose

Feedback data is used only to reply, troubleshoot problems, and improve Hue. It is not used for advertising, cross-app tracking, sale of data, or marketing profiles.

4. Retention and deletion

Feedback is retained for no more than 24 months and then removed by the developer. To request earlier deletion, use About > Feedback in Hue or email leona.soft@icloud.com, and include the email address or approximate submission time associated with the original feedback.

5. Apple services

Apple StoreKit processes the Hue Pro lifetime purchase and optional consumable tips. Hue treats only the lifetime product as a Pro entitlement; tips do not unlock features and cannot be restored. Notifications are scheduled locally on the device. CloudKit and StoreKit are also governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Website hosting

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process your IP address and technical request data for security and service operation under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Hue does not use this website access data for tracking or advertising.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or deletion requests, contact the developer through About > Feedback in Hue or email leona.soft@icloud.com.

Hue does not sell personal data or track people across apps.
